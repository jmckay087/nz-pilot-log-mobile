Sync to Excel in iCloud

This version keeps each step separate so iPhone Safari has a clear user tap for choosing, preparing and saving the workbook.
Tap Choose Excel workbook and select your current CAANZ Excel Pilot Logbook from iCloud Drive.
Tap Prepare update. NZ Pilot Log adds all waiting phone flights to the next safe logbook rows.
Tap Save updated workbook, choose Save to Files, then save over the same iCloud Drive workbook and choose Replace.
Return here and tap I replaced the iCloud workbook.
Do not confirm until the updated workbook has been saved back over the current iCloud copy.

Why the Mac will see it

Once iCloud Drive syncs the updated workbook to your Mac, NZ Pilot Log Mac v4.1+ scans the workbook and imports the new rows into its local database. You do not need the phone and Mac app open at the same time.
